# 🛡️ WARE Framework v2.0: The Enterprise Risk Protocol

## 🏛️ Comprehensive Strategic Blueprint & Infrastructure Map

---

## 💎 Core Philosophy

WARE is an **Internal Sovereign Risk Protocol** where:

- **ML Discovers**  
  Acts as an *Expert Witness*, providing narrative context for anomalies.

- **Deterministic Core Decides**  
  The judicial engine with domain-specific deterministic grammar.

- **Humans Govern**  
  Policy sovereignty remains with humans; all *Rule Births* require explicit human approval.

---

## 🧱 The 6 Pillars of v2.0 Infrastructure

---

### 1. Internal Sovereign Core (Domain-Specific Grammar)

- **Architecture**  
  The risk brain resides entirely within the internal data warehouse.

- **Grammar**  
  Each domain (Login, Payment, etc.) operates under its own deterministic grammar, governed by a shared constitutional framework to prevent architectural drift.

---

### 2. Narrative ML & Data Reliability Framework

- **Explainable ML**  
  Every flag must be a human-readable narrative, not merely a statistical score.

- **Data Reliability Governor**
  - **Core Concept**: Reliability is a *Governor (Authority)*, not a multiplier.  
    It determines the *severity of the verdict*  
    (e.g., High Reliability → Step-up Authentication, Low Reliability → Hard Block).

  - **Reliability Ladder**  
    Users graduate through tiers  
    *(New Born → Emerging → Established → Mature → Veteran)*  
    based on a matrix of **Account Age** and **Successful Transaction Count**.

- **Anti-Exploit Guardrails**
  - **Anti-Burst**: Graduation requires activity distributed over *X distinct days*.
  - **Trust Decay**: Reliability erodes after 90 days of inactivity to prevent *Sleeper 2.0* exploits.

- **Rule Lifecycle**  
  Discovery (ML) → Shadow Mode Simulation (Backtesting) → Sovereignty (Human Approval)

---

### 3. Federated Risk Budgeting & Global Arbitration

- **Domain Autonomy**  
  Separate risk budgets for Login, Payment, and Promo Abuse.

- **Global Ceiling**  
  A unified enterprise-level risk ceiling governed by a pre-approved arbitration function.

- **W₂ Floor Constraint**  
  Global fraud patterns always retain a minimum weight to prevent highly reliable users from bypassing systemic attacks (e.g., BIN or network-wide fraud).

---

### 4. Time-Travel & Intelligence Loop

- **Immutable Decisions**  
  Every action is stored with its exact logic version and policy snapshot.

- **Learning Path**  
  The Replay Engine generates *Policy Improvement Signals* from historical data, without affecting the live execution path.

---

### 5. Constitutional Constraints & Conflict Resolution

- **Signature Layer**  
  Non-negotiable constraints (e.g., anti-discrimination, hard caps on false positives).

- **Absolute Override**  
  Constitutional breaches (Sanctions / Anti-Terrorism) trigger immediate Hard Blocks regardless of user reliability.

- **Conflict Resolution**  
  In case of constraint conflicts, the system fails closed and escalates to dual human approval with recorded rationale.

---

### 6. Governance & Audit Readiness

- **Named Ownership**  
  Every policy change is linked to a human owner with timestamped justification.

- **Explicit Governance Matrix**  
  The relationship between Risk Scores, Reliability Levels, and Final Verdicts is stored in a reference table for audit transparency.

- **Evidence Infrastructure**  
  Built-in capability to explain any historical decision to regulators or auditors.
